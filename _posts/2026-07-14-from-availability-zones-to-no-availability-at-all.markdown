If you came here for your daily dose of Oracle Multicloud networking deep dives, I have bad news: this blog is about to get a lot less useful for you.

After more than 9 years at Oracle, July 15, 2026 is my last working day. Starting in September, my partner and I are heading off on a round-the-world trip for roughly the next nine months. The plan is loose on purpose: long hikes, slow travel, and taking the time to mountaineer, surf and see what's out there. 



I haven't set up any social media for the trip yet (how does that work?), so this post is the placeholder. Occasional updates will land here whenever I have decent internet and something worth sharing. Expect fewer private endpoints and route tables, more mountains and questionable campsite decisions.

Regular tech content will resume around mid-2027.
